Privacy Policy

At Wahab Jewellers, located in Liberty Market, Gulberg III, Lahore, we prioritize the confidentiality and security of our client's personal details. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard your data during consultations and website interactions.

1. Information We Collect

We only gather personal data voluntarily submitted when booking private store visits or requesting custom CAD designs, such as your full name, phone number/WhatsApp contact, and preferred jewelry interest.

2. Security & Confidentiality

Your personal records and bespoke design orders are held with absolute discretion. We do not sell, rent, or lease client lists to third parties under any circumstances.

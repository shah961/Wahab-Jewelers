1. Gold Rate Fluctuations & Pricing

Gold prices quoted on invoices or during custom order bookings are tied directly to the official daily market rates published by the Lahore Sarafa Association. Final prices are locked upon receipt of advance deposit confirmation.

2. Custom Bespoke Orders

All custom 3D CAD modeled bridal orders require a minimum non-refundable advance deposit. Due to the tailor-made nature of custom jewelry, non-fault returns or exchanges are strictly subject to management approval and standard gold buyback terms.
